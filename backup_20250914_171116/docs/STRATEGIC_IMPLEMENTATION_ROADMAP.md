## Strategic Considerations & Risk Mitigation

### Challenge 1: The "Quantum" Hurdle
**Risk**: Enterprise skepticism due to AI/quantum hype

**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Lead with business problems, not technology
- Use "quantum-computational advantage" terminology
- Provide head-to-head benchmarks vs classical solvers (Gurobi, CPLEX)
- Leverage LTC for undeniable evidence and audit trails

### Challenge 2: Dual-Audience Messaging
**Risk**: Data scientists use the platform, but executives buy it

**Mitigation Strategy**:
- **For Data Scientists**: Technical deep dives, API docs, QRBM white papers
- **For Executives**: ROI-focused one-pagers, risk reduction metrics
- **For Compliance**: LTC audit trails and regulatory compliance documentation

### Challenge 3: Classical Competition
**Risk**: "Why not improved classical algorithms on AWS?"

**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Target highly non-convex solution spaces
- Focus on combinatorial explosion scenarios (10,000+ dynamic constraints)
- Demonstrate classical solver inflection points in POV
- Emphasize time-to-solution and quality-of-solution advantages

### Challenge 4: Sales Motion Scalability
**Risk**: Workshop+POV model is resource-intensive

**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Develop "Value Assessment" qualification tools
- Create standardized technical assessment processes
- Build repeatable POV frameworks
