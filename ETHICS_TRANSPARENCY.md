# Ethics, Explainability & Transparency

## Responsible AI Charter
- Fairness, accountability, and transparency by design (exclusive to our NQBA™)
- Continuous bias assessment and mitigation
- Open benchmarking of AI/quantum models

## Explainability
- All AI decisions traceable and auditable
- Explainable outputs for every client-facing module

## Transparency
- Public model cards for all core pods
- Disclosure of data sources and limitations

## Ongoing Oversight
- Client and community advisory boards
- Regular publication of ethical impact reports
