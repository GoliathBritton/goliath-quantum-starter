# Platform Architecture & Modular Design

## Overview
Goliath Quantum, FLYFOX AI, and Sigma Select deliver a microservices-based, API-first platform that integrates proprietary, quantum-inspired AI modules, business workflow automation, and an open marketplace for rapid enterprise adoption—exclusively on our NQBA™ foundation.

## Key Components

### 1. AI Pods (Microservices)
- **qdLLM Engine**: Quantum LLM reasoning, exclusive to our platform
- **QNLP Processor**: Quantum NLP with semantic entanglement (proprietary)
- **QTransformers**: Quantum-enhanced transformers for classification, prediction, and more
- **Custom Pods**: Industry or client-specific modules, delivered under our brands

### 2. NQBA™ Platform Orchestration
- API gateway for secure, intelligent routing and scaling
- Intelligent resource allocation (NQBA-driven)
- Event-driven workflow engine

### 3. Integration Layers
- REST/gRPC APIs for all pods
- Pre-built connectors for ERP, CRM, EHR, financial systems, etc.
- SDKs for Python, JS, Java

### 4. Deployment Modes
- Public/private cloud, on-premises, edge/hybrid—always secured, always under our brands
- K8s-native, Docker-ready, and serverless support

### 5. Marketplace
- Discover, deploy, and upgrade pods and extensions—all guaranteed compliant with the Goliath/FLYFOX/Sigma Select ecosystem
- Partner and third-party module support (with strict branding requirements)

## Security & Compliance
- End-to-end encryption, RBAC, auditability
- Built-in compliance controls (HIPAA, SOC2, GDPR)
- All compliance certifications reference our company brands

## Monitoring & Observability
- Centralized dashboard
- Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry integration

## Extensibility
- Open APIs, SDKs, and event hooks for third-party innovation—subject to our branding and IP guidelines
