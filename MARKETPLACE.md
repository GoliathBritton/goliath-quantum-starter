# Goliath Quantum Marketplace & Ecosystem

## Marketplace Vision
A one-stop marketplace for AI pods, extensions, datasets, and integrations, enabling clients and partners to customize and extend the platform—always within the Goliath, FLYFOX AI, and Sigma Select ecosystem and under our NQBA™ foundation.

## Marketplace Features
- **Pod Catalog**: Browse and deploy pre-built AI/quantum modules (NQBA™-certified)
- **Extension Store**: Third-party and partner-built plugins (reviewed and branded for ecosystem fit)
- **Industry Solutions**: Pre-packaged workflows for key verticals
- **Data Exchange**: Curated datasets for training and fine-tuning
- **Marketplace APIs**: For automation and CI/CD

## Developer & Partner Ecosystem
- **Partner Certification**: Enable trusted integrators and resellers (brand compliance required)
- **Revenue Sharing**: For third-party modules and datasets
- **Community Contributions**: Open submission and review process (subject to IP and branding policy)

## Roadmap
- Q1: Launch core marketplace with official pods
- Q2: Open to third-party developers
- Q3: Enable in-platform purchases and upgrades
