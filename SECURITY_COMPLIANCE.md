# Security, Privacy & Compliance Roadmap

## Security Principles
- Zero-trust architecture, least-privilege, RBAC
- End-to-end encryption in transit and at rest
- Regular vulnerability scans and penetration tests

## Compliance Roadmap
- SOC2, HIPAA, GDPR baseline (certified for Goliath, FLYFOX AI, Sigma Select clients)
- Industry-specific certifications (PCI DSS, FedRAMP, etc.)
- Automated audit logging and reporting

## Privacy & Data Residency
- Data minimization and anonymization tools
- Regional data residency controls

## Client Transparency
- Real-time compliance dashboards
- Explainable AI outputs for audits
