# Partnership Tiers & Benefits

## Overview

The Goliath Quantum Platform Partner Program is designed to create a thriving ecosystem of technology providers, solution integrators, and industry specialists who can leverage our quantum computing capabilities to deliver value to their clients. This document outlines the partnership tiers, benefits, requirements, and application process.

## Partnership Vision

Our partnership program aims to:

1. Accelerate quantum adoption across industries
2. Enable partners to differentiate their offerings with quantum capabilities
3. Create a collaborative ecosystem that drives innovation
4. Provide clear pathways for mutual business growth
5. Deliver exceptional value to end customers through combined expertise

## Partnership Tiers

### Tier 1: Technology Alliance Partner

**Description:** For technology providers who want to integrate with the Goliath Quantum Platform to extend their existing solutions with quantum capabilities.

**Requirements:**
- Completed technical integration with Goliath Quantum Platform
- Dedicated technical resource for integration maintenance
- Joint marketing commitment (minimum 2 activities per year)
- Annual partnership fee: $5,000

**Benefits:**
- API access with higher rate limits (10,000 calls/day)
- Technical documentation and integration support
- Partner portal access
- Listing in partner directory (basic)
- Co-marketing opportunities (blog posts, case studies)
- 10% discount on platform usage for partner's internal use
- Quarterly business reviews

### Tier 2: Solution Partner

**Description:** For consulting firms and system integrators who implement Goliath Quantum solutions for their clients.

**Requirements:**
- Minimum of 2 certified Goliath Quantum specialists
- Completion of partner enablement program
- Minimum of 3 client implementations per year
- Joint marketing commitment (minimum 4 activities per year)
- Annual partnership fee: $15,000

**Benefits:**
- All Technology Alliance Partner benefits
- API access with premium rate limits (25,000 calls/day)
- Enhanced listing in partner directory (featured)
- Access to solution accelerators and templates
- Joint customer success stories
- 15% discount on platform usage for partner's internal use
- 10% referral fee on client implementations
- Dedicated partner manager
- Priority technical support
- Quarterly business and technical reviews

### Tier 3: Strategic Partner

**Description:** For organizations deeply committed to quantum computing innovation who want to co-develop solutions and go-to-market strategies.

**Requirements:**
- Minimum of 5 certified Goliath Quantum specialists
- Executive sponsorship and strategic alignment
- Joint business plan with revenue targets
- Minimum of 5 client implementations per year
- Joint marketing commitment (minimum 8 activities per year)
- Annual partnership fee: $50,000

**Benefits:**
- All Solution Partner benefits
- API access with unlimited rate limits
- Premium listing in partner directory (top placement)
- Early access to new features and capabilities
- Co-development opportunities
- Joint solution development fund (matching up to $100,000)
- 25% discount on platform usage for partner's internal use
- 15% referral fee on client implementations
- Executive sponsorship and quarterly executive reviews
- Dedicated technical account manager
- Invitation to partner advisory board
- Co-branded marketing materials
- Joint roadmap planning sessions

## Technical Certification

### Goliath Quantum Certified Specialist

**Requirements:**
- Completion of Goliath Quantum technical training (online or in-person)
- Passing score on certification exam (80% or higher)
- Practical implementation project
- Annual recertification

**Benefits:**
- Official certification badge and certificate
- Listing in certified professionals directory
- Access to technical resources and community
- Invitation to specialist-only events and webinars

### Goliath Quantum Certified Architect

**Requirements:**
- Goliath Quantum Certified Specialist status
- Advanced architecture training completion
- Passing score on architect exam (85% or higher)
- Minimum of 3 successful implementations
- Case study submission
- Annual recertification

**Benefits:**
- All Certified Specialist benefits
- Advanced architect badge and certificate
- Priority access to technical support
- Invitation to architect roundtables
- Early access to roadmap and beta features
- Speaking opportunities at Goliath Quantum events
