# Co-Marketing Guidelines

## Overview

This document outlines the guidelines, processes, and best practices for co-marketing activities between Goliath Quantum and our partners. These guidelines ensure consistent brand representation, maximize marketing impact, and create mutual value through collaborative promotional efforts.

## Co-Marketing Objectives

1. Increase market awareness of joint solutions
2. Generate qualified leads for both parties
3. Establish thought leadership in quantum computing applications
4. Accelerate customer adoption through combined value propositions
5. Strengthen partner relationships through successful collaboration

- **Joint White Papers**
  - Technical deep dives on quantum solutions
  - Industry-specific application guides
  - Research reports and market analyses

- **Case Studies**
  - Customer success stories
  - Implementation examples
  - ROI and business impact analyses

- **Blog Posts**
  - Thought leadership articles
  - Technical tutorials
  - Industry insights

### Digital Marketing

- **Webinars**

- **Social Media Campaigns**
  - Coordinated announcements
  - Shared content promotion
  - Engagement activities

- **Email Marketing**

### Events

- **Trade Shows & Conferences**
  - Shared booth presence

  - Co-sponsored networking events

- **Partner Workshops**
  - Technical training sessions

  - Industry-focused discussions

## Branding Guidelines

### Logo Usage

- Partners may use the Goliath Quantum logo only in approved co-marketing materials
- The logo must be used according to our brand guidelines
- Logo files are available in the partner portal
- All logo usage must be approved by the Goliath Quantum marketing team

### Messaging

- Key messaging points are provided in the partner portal
- Partners should align with Goliath Quantum's positioning and value proposition
- Industry-specific messaging templates are available for customization
- All public-facing messaging must be approved by both parties

### Visual Identity

- Co-branded materials should follow the visual identity guidelines
- Templates for presentations, white papers, and digital assets are provided
- The visual hierarchy should reflect the nature of the partnership
- Design support is available for strategic partners

## Co-Marketing Process

### Planning Phase

1. **Opportunity Identification**
   - Partners identify co-marketing opportunities
   - Submission through partner portal
   - Initial evaluation by partner manager

2. **Proposal Development**
   - Define objectives and target audience
   - Outline activities and timeline
   - Estimate resource requirements
   - Establish success metrics

3. **Approval Process**
   - Review by Goliath Quantum marketing team
   - Partner marketing team approval
   - Budget allocation
   - Final sign-off by both parties

### Execution Phase

1. **Content Creation**
   - Collaborative content development
   - Review and approval cycles
   - Final production
   - Asset management in shared repository

2. **Campaign Launch**
   - Coordinated timing
   - Cross-promotion in respective channels
   - Consistent tracking implementation
   - Regular status updates

3. **Ongoing Management**
   - Weekly coordination calls
   - Real-time performance monitoring
   - Agile adjustments as needed
   - Issue resolution process

### Evaluation Phase

1. **Performance Measurement**
   - Data collection from all channels
   - Comparison against established KPIs
   - Attribution analysis
   - ROI calculation

2. **Results Sharing**
   - Joint performance review
   - Documentation of outcomes
   - Lessons learned
   - Success stories

3. **Future Planning**
   - Identification of follow-up opportunities
   - Refinement of approach
   - Long-term collaboration roadmap

## Resource Allocation

### Financial Investment

- **Tier 1 Partners**: 50/50 cost sharing up to $5,000 per quarter
- **Tier 2 Partners**: 60/40 cost sharing (Goliath/Partner) up to $10,000 per quarter
- **Tier 3 Partners**: 70/30 cost sharing (Goliath/Partner) up to $25,000 per quarter
- Additional funding available for high-impact strategic initiatives

### Personnel Resources

- Goliath Quantum will provide:
  - Partner marketing manager
  - Content development support
  - Design resources
  - Technical SMEs for content validation

- Partners are expected to provide:
  - Marketing point of contact
  - Subject matter experts
  - Content review and approval
  - Channel access for distribution

### Asset Development

- Standard assets provided by Goliath Quantum:
  - Logo files and brand guidelines
  - Product images and screenshots
  - Boilerplate descriptions
  - Technical diagrams

- Custom asset development:
  - Available based on partnership tier
  - Subject to approval process
  - Requires joint investment
  - Ownership defined in partnership agreement

## Best Practices

### Successful Co-Marketing Examples

- Case Study: Financial Services Solution
  - Joint webinar with 500+ attendees
  - Technical white paper with 1,000+ downloads
  - 25 qualified leads generated

- Case Study: Healthcare Implementation
  - Conference speaking session
  - Press release with industry coverage
  - Executive roundtable with 15 prospects

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- Inconsistent messaging across channels
- Delayed approval processes
- Unclear lead handling procedures
- Insufficient tracking mechanisms
- Imbalanced resource commitment

### Tips for Success

- Start planning at least 8 weeks before launch
- Establish clear roles and responsibilities
- Create a shared calendar for all activities
- Implement consistent tracking from day one
- Schedule regular check-ins throughout the campaign
- Document and share lessons learned
