## Technology Overview

### Neuromorphic Quantum Computing Architecture

Dynex's patent-pending neuromorphic quantum computing utilizes ion drifting of electrons, operating differently from traditional superconducting-based quantum computing. <mcreference link="https://dynexcoin.medium.com/neuromorphic-quantum-computing-dynexs-ion-drift-quantum-circuits-b6c8cac30d67" index="5">5</mcreference> The platform employs physical dynamical systems and exploits time non-locality (memory) to compute efficiently, overcoming the exponentially increasing complexity of Schrödinger's equations.

**Key Technical Advantages:**
- Linear complexity scaling vs. exponential growth in traditional quantum systems
- Digital Twin simulation of physical neuromorphic quantum machines
- Operation on hundreds of thousands of GPUs in parallel <mcreference link="https://dynexcoin.medium.com/resilienture-using-dynex-neuromorphic-quantum-computing-to-accelerate-coastal-resilience-b646a40129e7" index="2">2</mcreference>
