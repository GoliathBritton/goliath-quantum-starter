# Revenue Sharing Model

## Overview

This document outlines the revenue sharing model for Goliath Quantum Platform partners. It defines the financial structure for various partnership types, revenue streams, and the processes for tracking, reporting, and distributing shared revenue.

## Revenue Sharing Principles

1. **Mutual Value Creation**: Our model ensures both Goliath Quantum and partners benefit from joint success
2. **Transparency**: Clear definitions, calculations, and reporting processes
3. **Performance-Based**: Higher rewards for partners who drive more value
4. **Simplicity**: Easy to understand and implement
5. **Scalability**: Designed to accommodate growth in partnership volume and complexity

## Partner Revenue Sharing Models

### Referral Partners

**Eligibility**: Partners who refer customers to Goliath Quantum but are not involved in implementation or ongoing support.

**Revenue Share Structure**:
- 10% of first-year subscription revenue
- One-time payment for perpetual licenses (10% of license value)
- No share of professional services revenue

**Payment Terms**:
- Paid quarterly, 30 days after the end of the quarter
- Minimum payment threshold: $500
- 12-month referral attribution period

**Requirements**:
- Registered opportunity in partner portal
- Confirmed customer attribution
- Signed referral agreement

### Implementation Partners

**Eligibility**: Partners who implement Goliath Quantum solutions for customers and provide level 1 support.

**Revenue Share Structure**:
- 15% of first-year subscription revenue
- 5% of renewal revenue for years 2-3
- One-time payment for perpetual licenses (15% of license value)
- Professional services revenue split based on delivery responsibility

**Payment Terms**:
- Paid quarterly, 30 days after the end of the quarter
- Minimum payment threshold: $1,000
- 24-month customer attribution period

**Requirements**:
- Minimum of 2 certified specialists
- Signed implementation partner agreement
- Customer satisfaction score of 4+ (out of 5)

### Reseller Partners

**Eligibility**: Partners who resell Goliath Quantum solutions as part of their portfolio.

**Revenue Share Structure**:
- 25% discount on list price (reseller margin)
- Additional 5% performance incentive for exceeding quarterly targets
- Volume-based additional discounts:
  - $100K-$250K quarterly: additional 2%
  - $250K-$500K quarterly: additional 3%
  - $500K+ quarterly: additional 5%

**Payment Terms**:
- Invoiced at standard partner terms (net 30)
- Performance incentives paid quarterly
- Volume incentives calculated on rolling 4-quarter basis

**Requirements**:
- Signed reseller agreement
- Minimum quarterly revenue commitment
- Maintenance of partner certification requirements

### Technology Partners

**Eligibility**: Partners who integrate their technology with Goliath Quantum and drive joint sales.

**Revenue Share Structure**:
- 10% of Goliath Quantum revenue from joint customers
- Revenue share on partner products sold to Goliath Quantum customers (negotiated case-by-case)
- Joint solution development fund contributions based on partnership tier

**Payment Terms**:
- Paid quarterly, 45 days after the end of the quarter
- Minimum payment threshold: $1,000
- 36-month customer attribution period

**Requirements**:
- Completed technical integration
- Joint go-to-market plan
- Signed technology partner agreement

## Qualified Revenue Definitions

### Included Revenue Types

- **Subscription Licenses**: All recurring subscription fees
- **Perpetual Licenses**: One-time license purchases
- **Maintenance & Support**: Annual support contracts
- **Professional Services**: Implementation, training, and consulting services
- **Add-on Modules**: Additional platform capabilities
- **Usage-Based Fees**: Consumption-based charges

### Excluded Revenue Types

- **Taxes and Regulatory Fees**: Any government-imposed charges
- **Third-Party Pass-Through Costs**: External technology costs
- **Hardware**: Physical equipment sales
- **Discounts and Credits**: Any reductions from list price
- **Refunded Amounts**: Any returned payments

## Revenue Attribution Rules

### Customer Attribution

- First partner to register opportunity receives attribution
- 72-hour response time for attribution disputes
- Executive review process for contested attributions
- Multi-partner attribution allowed with proportional revenue share

### Deal Registration Process

1. Partner submits opportunity in partner portal
2. Goliath Quantum reviews for conflicts (2 business days)
3. Opportunity approved or rejected with explanation
4. 90-day protection period with option for extension

### Attribution Periods

- **Referral Partners**: 12 months from first purchase
- **Implementation Partners**: 24 months from first purchase
- **Reseller Partners**: Duration of reseller relationship
- **Technology Partners**: 36 months from first purchase

## Reporting and Payment Process

### Revenue Tracking

- Partner-attributed deals tagged in CRM
- Monthly preliminary reports available in partner portal
- Quarterly final reports generated for payment processing
- Annual review of attribution and payment accuracy

### Payment Schedule

- Quarterly payments processed within 30-45 days of quarter end
- Electronic payments via ACH or wire transfer
- Detailed remittance advice provided with each payment
- Annual true-up process for any discrepancies

### Reporting Access

- Real-time pipeline visibility in partner portal
- Monthly revenue forecasts
- Quarterly payment statements
- Annual partnership performance reviews

## Performance Tiers and Incentives

### Performance Measurement

- Quarterly revenue targets established annually
- Performance measured against targets and year-over-year growth
- Customer satisfaction and retention metrics included
- Technical certification compliance monitored

### Tier Advancement

- **Bronze Tier**: Entry level, standard revenue share
- **Silver Tier**: 125% of target, +2% revenue share
- **Gold Tier**: 150% of target, +3% revenue share
- **Platinum Tier**: 200% of target, +5% revenue share

### Additional Incentives

- **Deal Accelerator Bonus**: +2% for deals closed in <60 days
- **New Logo Bonus**: +3% for new customer acquisitions
- **Strategic Industry Bonus**: +2% for priority industry wins
- **Multi-Year Contract Bonus**: +1% per additional year

## Governance and Dispute Resolution

### Governance Committee

- Quarterly review of revenue sharing program
- Approval of policy exceptions
- Resolution of escalated disputes
- Annual program evaluation and updates

### Dispute Resolution Process

1. Initial review by partner manager
2. Escalation to partnership operations team
3. Final determination by governance committee
4. Binding arbitration option for unresolved disputes

### Program Changes

- 90-day notice for any material program changes
- Grandfathering provisions for existing deals
- Partner consultation for significant model revisions
- Annual review and update cycle

## Legal and Compliance Requirements

### Contractual Requirements

- Master partner agreement must be executed
- Revenue sharing addendum with specific terms
- Compliance with anti-bribery and corruption policies
- Adherence to data protection regulations

### Tax Considerations

- Partners responsible for applicable taxes on revenue share
- Tax documentation requirements by jurisdiction
- Annual tax reporting provided
- International tax considerations addressed in agreements

### Audit Rights

- Goliath Quantum reserves right to audit partner records
- Partners may request revenue calculation audit
- Third-party audit option for unresolved discrepancies
- 24-month lookback period for corrections
